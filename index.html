About me

Hi, I'm Amit Sharma, a Data Analyst with an MA in Business Economics. I enjoy turning raw, messy data into insights people can actually use. I'm the sort of person who keeps digging until I find the pattern a dataset is trying to show me.

I've completed various projects spanning retail, government, real estate, transportation, customer service, and personal data, including segmenting e-commerce customers to guide retention strategy, turning years of government workforce data into projections and a policy briefing for a diversity steering committee, building an interactive sales dashboard from scratch in Excel, and rebuilding my own Spotify history as a dynamic Tableau dashboard. Good analysis, to me, is as much about asking the right question as it is about finding the answer.

I've also taken that same curiosity into research. My recent, data-driven study, Economic Implications of Armed Conflict, examines how armed conflict impacts economies in the Middle East and North Africa, using education systems as the lens.
